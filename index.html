Qalam Hub

A place where your ideas get written down

Qalam Hub is a coworking space in the center of Astana. We give freelancers, students and small teams a calm and well equipped place to work: fast internet, comfortable desks, meeting rooms and free coffee. Come for a day or stay for a month, your desk is always ready.

Book a seat

Why Qalam Hub

  • Fast internet

    Fiber connection at 500 Mbps with a backup line, stable for calls and heavy uploads.

  • Open 24/7

    Residents get a personal access card and can work at any hour, including weekends.

  • Free coffee

    Unlimited coffee, tea and filtered water in the kitchen for every visitor.

  • Community events

    Weekly meetups, lectures and networking evenings for residents at no extra cost.

Our halls

Open space

60 desks in a light hall with panoramic windows. Choose any free seat or reserve a fixed one with your own locker.

Meeting rooms

Three rooms for 4 to 12 people with screens and whiteboards. Booking by the hour, free for team tariffs.

Quiet zone

Single booths for deep focus work and online calls. Soundproof walls, a lamp and a comfortable chair.
